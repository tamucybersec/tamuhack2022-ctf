# Gerardo's Bio 1

## Description 

Gerardo recently started using twitter [twitter](https://twitter.com/gerardo_2001_), but he hasn't filled out his bio yet. Let's start off simple. Can you help him out by adding his birthday? 

The flag is in the format of gigem{MM/DD/YYYY}

## Solution
In this introduction to OSINT challenge, everything needed to find Gerardo’s birthday is present on his timeline. Gerardo has a total of 12 tweets, and a quick scroll through his timeline will allow us to find a [tweet](https://twitter.com/gerardo_2001_/status/1474055931138871306?s=20) on December 23rd of him wishing himself a happy birthday. Then, using the only mention of a year in his twitter handle, gerardo\_**2001**\_, we can assume Gerardo’s birthday is December 23rd, 2001. Thus, the flag is `gigem{12/23/2001}`.
